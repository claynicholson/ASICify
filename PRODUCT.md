# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hardware engineers, ML engineers at edge-AI companies, and chip-startup founders evaluating whether to trust an open-source compiler with a tape-out decision that costs $5-30M. They read datasheets for a living, are allergic to marketing fluff, and judge credibility by whether the numbers are real and the methodology is cited. Secondary audience: open-source contributors browsing for a serious project.

## Product Purpose

ASICify is an open (MIT) compiler that turns trained PyTorch models into hardware-ready artifacts: synthesizable Verilog with hardwired weights, Cocotb testbenches, and area/cost/throughput estimates across eleven hardware targets. The site's job is to establish engineering credibility in the first viewport and get visitors into the live playground, where real math runs in their browser.

## Brand Personality

Precise, verifiable, engineered. The voice of a well-set foundry datasheet: every number real, every claim cited, zero decoration that doesn't inform. Confidence through specificity ("8.2 mm², $4.10 @ 100K units"), never through superlatives.

## Anti-references

- The AI-startup dark-mode-with-neon-gradients template.
- The cream-paper editorial-notebook aesthetic with hand-drawn ornaments, rotated stamps, and wobble animations (the site's previous look; it read as vibecoded).
- Generic SaaS: icon-card grids, hero-metric blocks, gradient text, fake terminal traffic lights.

## Design Principles

1. Show the artifact, not an illustration of it. Imagery is real product output: floorplans, terminal transcripts, spec tables with real numbers.
2. Datasheet grammar as the design system: numbered sections, hairline rules, a strict visible grid, mono for data.
3. One committed type family (Archivo) with strong weight/width contrast; mono strictly for data and code.
4. Copper accent used decisively in figures and interactive states, never as wallpaper.
5. Every number on the page must be a real number from the cost model.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text. Focus-visible rings on every interactive element. No information carried by color alone. Reduced-motion respected (site has essentially no entrance motion by design).
